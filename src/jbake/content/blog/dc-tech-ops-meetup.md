On Tuesday night, my meetup group ([DC Continuous Integration, Delivery and Deployment Group](https://www.meetup.com/DC-continuous-integration/ "DC Continuous Integration, Delivery, & Deployment Group")) joined forces with a number of other groups to present the most awesome gathering we have ever known.  The meeting was the brainchild of [Nathen Harvey](http://www.nathenharvey.com/ "Nathen Harvey"), who runs the [DevOpsDC](https://www.meetup.com/DevOpsDC/ "DevOpsDC") and [MongoDC](https://web.archive.org/web/20130309132026/http://www.meetup.com/Washington-DC-MongoDB-Users-Group/ "MongoDC") user groups and works for [OpsCode](https://www.chef.io/ "OpsCode") as their Technical Community Manager.  Nathen sent an email with the subject of 'DC Tech Ops Meetup Alliance (aka the Super Friends)'...how could I not be in.

[The](https://www.meetup.com/DevOpsDC/events/97686352/ "DevOpsDC Meeting Invite") meeting brought together [Big Data DC](https://web.archive.org/web/20130126052131/http://www.meetup.com:80/bigdatadc/ "Big Data DC"), [Crabby Admins - LOPSA, Baltimore Chapter](http://crabbyadmins.org/ "Crabby Admins"), [DC Agile Software Testing Group (DCAST)](https://www.meetup.com/D-CAST/ "DCAST"), [DC Continuous Integration, Delivery, & Deployment Group](https://www.meetup.com/DC-continuous-integration/ "DC Continuous Integration, Delivery and Deployment Group"), [DevOpsDC](https://www.meetup.com/DevOpsDC/ "DevOpsDC"), [LOPSA DC - The League of Professional System Administrators](https://web.archive.org/web/20131207194945/http://www.meetup.com/lopsadc/ "LOPSA"), [NOVA MySQL](https://web.archive.org/web/20130131065633/http://www.meetup.com:80/NOVA-MySQL/ "NOVA MySQL"), [Arlington Ruby](https://www.meetup.com/Arlington-Ruby/ "Arlington Ruby") and [DC Ruby User Group](https://web.archive.org/web/20130214214702/http://www.dcrug.org:80/ "DC Ruby User Group").

For the meeting, we brought in some great speakers.  First, we had [Avleen Vig](https://x.com/avleen "Avleen Vig") and [Patrick McDonnell](https://web.archive.org/web/20130919041448/https://twitter.com/mcdonnps "Patrick McDonnell") from [Etsy](http://www.etsy.com/ "Etsy") to talk about [Ops School](http://www.opsschool.org/en/latest/ "OpsSchool").  Here's the video:

[Video: Ops School](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0gx7oQaAy6g)

After Avleen and Patrick, [Theo Schlossnagle](https://web.archive.org/web/20130306022941/http://omniti.com/is/theo-schlossnagle "Theo Schlossnagle"), CEO of [OmniTI](https://web.archive.org/web/20130302092531/http://omniti.com/ "OmniTI")  was up next.  We were lucky enough that he gave two presentations.  The first was a short presentation on How to Tie Your Shoes the Right Way (I love the unicorns):

[Video: How To Tie Your Shoes The Right Way](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UQ6UNhdP6Ic)

Next, Theo gave a great presentation on careers.  It was spot on and definitely worth watching.  Here's the video:

[Video: a career](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qyxUIY72ywc)

I want to send a special thank you to Nathen for making this all happen.  It was a great night.  I definitely look forward to further collaborations between DevOpsDc and my meetup.  If you are looking for the original meeting invite, here it is.  Enjoy the videos.
