I haven't posted in a while, but I wanted to share some upcoming talks I will be giving before the end of the new year.

<img align="right" title="agiledc" alt="I'm speaking at AgileDC, October 23, 2012" src="/img/agiledc.png" height="100" width="242" />Tomorrow I will closing the day at [AgileDC](https://web.archive.org/web/20121013074043/http://agiledc.org:80/ "AgileDC") with my [Continuous Delivery Applied](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/continuous-delivery-applied/14204775 "Continuous Delivery Applied presenation") presentation.  This will be my first time attending AgileDC and I am very excited to be ask to present.  I've made some updates and improvements to my talk, so come out and see it again.  I am looking forward to it and I hope to see everybody there.

<img align="left" title="agile-richmond" alt="Agile Richmond logo" src="/img/agile-richmond.jpg" height="80" width="210" />On November 14, I will continue my speaking "tour" in Richmond.  The great folks at [Agile Richmond](https://web.archive.org/web/20121116035327/http://www.agilerichmond.org:80/ "Agile Richmond") have asked me to come down and give my **Continuous Delivery Applied** presentation.  I look forward to heading south, and meeting up with some good friends while I am there as well.

<img align="right" title="excella" alt="Excella Consulting logo" src="/img/excella.png" height="140" width="181" />On December 7th, I will be participating in [Excella Consulting](https://www.excella.com/ "Excella Consulting")'s first conference, the DC Agile Engineering Conference.  This event is a one-day single track conference that takes place the day before the [Global Day of Coderetreat](https://www.coderetreat.org/ "Global Day of Coderetreat").  For this conference, I will collaborating on 2 separate talks.  In the first talk, [Sam Brown](https://web.archive.org/web/20200509225511/https://twitter.com/SamuelBrownIV "Samuel Brown") and I will be talking about **Improving Designs through TDD**.  In this talk we will share our experiences using Test Driven Development to improve the design of our code.  In the second talk, [Roberto Hernandez](https://x.com/hernandezrobert "Roberto Hernandez") and I will discuss Continuous Delivery.  Both Roberto and I have been speaking about **Continuous Delivery** for some time, so this talk should be a lot of fun.

I hope to see each and every one of you at one or all of my talks.
