Last December, I was invited to speak at the first annual [DC Agile Engineering Conference](https://web.archive.org/web/20121219020151/http://dc-agile-engineering-conference.eventbrite.com:80/ "DC Agile Engineering Conference").  For this conference, I collaborated with a former colleague of mine, [Sam Brown](https://web.archive.org/web/20200509225511/https://twitter.com/SamuelBrownIV "Sam Brown IV") on one of my talks.  Sam and I are both proponents of Test Driven Development and practiced it together on projects, so it made sense for us to talk about TDD.

The focus of our talk was on how Test Driven Development improves the design of an application.  We were lucky enough that our talk was recorded, and I would like to share this talk here with you today.
